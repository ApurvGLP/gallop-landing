How a Fortune Scale Global Retailer Achieved a 20x Effort Reduction in ERP Modernization with Gallop

← Case studies

Executive summary

For a leading global retailer, a critical Distributor Management System (DMS) built incrementally over 15 years had become a severe operational liability. Running on an aging, monolithic .NET and SQL Server architecture, the system stalled enterprise agility, demanded massive maintenance costs, and carried mounting compliance risks. Faced with a strict year-end modernization mandate, internal estimates projected a 60+ person-month effort, guaranteeing a missed deadline.

By adopting Gallop's AI-driven Modernization Platform, the retailer's IT leadership bypassed this high-risk trajectory. Equipped with Gallop's technology and supported by a single Gallop Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), the internal team took total control of the transformation. They achieved a 20x reduction in engineering effort, secured the critical deadline, and drastically lowered their Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).

The challenge: a 15-year-old monolith restricting growth

The client's entire distribution network relied on a deeply entrenched, inflexible legacy system. The IT organization faced four critical roadblocks:

  • Paralyzing technical debt: The core application relied on outdated .NET frameworks and thousands of complex, undocumented SQL Server stored procedures.
  • Stalled enterprise agility: Deploying minor logic updates required navigating extremely fragile code. Development cycles took months, stalling vital business initiatives and draining IT budgets.
  • Mounting operational risk: The aging architecture could no longer handle modern transaction volumes, leading to broken billing generation, data sync failures, and unreliable core features.
  • An impossible deadline: Internal teams estimated a 60+ person-month rewrite using traditional development methods and standard AI coding tools. Meeting the business-mandated year-end deadline was mathematically impossible.

The approach: engineer-led, AI-driven transformation

Rather than outsourcing the problem or triggering a multi-year internal rewrite, the client utilized the Gallop Platform to drive the modernization internally. The architectural overhaul was executed across four precise, de-risked phases:

  1. AI-assisted discovery: Gallop ingested 15 years of undocumented .NET code and deeply entangled SQL dependencies. In a matter of days, the platform automatically mapped user journeys and generated a verified Code-to-Logic map, creating a trustable, deterministic baseline.
  2. Agentic planning: To blueprint the future state, the client used Gallop's Planning Module to auto-generate complex technical specifications for the target Azure/PostgreSQL environment. Internal architects easily reviewed and approved these AI blueprints, ensuring total architectural control.
  3. AI-assisted execution: The internal team co-authored the new system with native AI agents. Gallop autonomously executed high-speed code refactoring and test generation on convoluted logic (like pricing matrices and statutory rules), allowing the internal engineers to focus strictly on strategic design and oversight.
  4. Reliability Mesh: To guarantee zero disruption for their 700+ active distributors, the team validated the new system using Gallop's automated Reliability Mesh. Exhaustive testing guaranteed 100% logic parity across every Critical User Journey (CUJ), enabling a zero-risk release.

Business impact: speed, safety, and ownership

By utilizing Gallop's AI platform, the client successfully broke free from a decade and a half of technical debt, fundamentally transforming their IT operations:

  • 20x reduction in engineering effort: A massive rewrite projected to take 60+ person-months was successfully delivered in just 3 person-months, utilizing minimal support from just one Gallop FDE.
  • Zero operational disruption: The transition was flawless. Over 700 distributor organizations were migrated with zero daily downtime and zero system failures.
  • Slashed IT maintenance costs: Retiring the on-prem .NET/SQL monolith eliminated exorbitant ongoing costs for hosting, patching, and maintaining outdated infrastructure.
  • Restored speed to market: Operating on a modern, decoupled Azure architecture, the internal team now fully owns their roadmap. They can instantly implement pricing changes, compliance updates, and AI demand forecasting on demand.

“Gallop's Modernization Platform empowered our internal team to conquer 15 years of technical debt in just three months, safely modernizing our entire Distributor Management System with zero disruption while restoring our enterprise agility.”
