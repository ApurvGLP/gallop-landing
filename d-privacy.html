1. Introduction

Gallop Intelligence Inc. (“Gallop”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data we process. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our AI-native SDLC platform and website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our enterprise partners and users:

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our platform, including diagnostic logs and performance metrics.
  • Account Information: Business contact details provided during the demo booking or consultation process.
  • System Metadata: Non-sensitive metadata from connected legacy repositories required for our AI analysis tools.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to facilitate the transformation of legacy systems and improve our autonomous agent performance:

  • To perform “Code-to-Logic” mapping and architectural analysis.
  • To train and refine our proprietary AI models for high-velocity refactoring (using anonymized or customer-specific isolated environments).
  • To communicate regarding system status, updates, and strategic consultations.

4. Data Security

Enterprise security is at the core of our thesis. We implement industry-leading encryption, multi-factor authentication, and strict access controls. Our systems are designed to operate within your secure perimeter or on isolated high-compliance cloud infrastructure.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact our team at team@gallopintelligence.ai
