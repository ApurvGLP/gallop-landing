The questions a CIO asks first

How is this different from a code translator?

Translators convert syntax and carry your legacy's flaws into a new language. Gallop comprehends the business logic first, what the system actually does, then re-engineers it natively. Comprehension, not translation.

How do I know the new system does everything the old one did?

Nothing reaches production on faith. The rebuild is validated in a sandbox, then deployed to your own UAT for your team to test, then run in parallel on limited traffic, proving parity against the live system before a single production cutover. You move forward only once that's proven.

Does this replace my team, or my SI?

Neither. Your team is put back in control. Gallop's agents do the heavy lifting. Your people make every decision on architecture, trade-offs, and cutover. The understanding stays in-house instead of leaving with the consultants.

Where does my code run? Is it secure?

Your code and business logic never leave your control. Deploy the Gallop Platform in your own cloud, on-prem, or fully air-gapped, with role-based access and a full audit trail on every extraction, decision, and change.

Do I have to commit to a full modernization to start?

No. Gallop Discover stands alone. It gives you deep comprehension plus a priced, agent-ready target architecture in weeks. You get a board-ready decision pack first. Everything after that is your choice.

Do you only modernize legacy systems, or build new ones too?

Both. The same platform that comprehends and rebuilds legacy systems can build net-new systems on a modern, agent-ready foundation. Replacing something twenty years old and building something that doesn't exist yet use the same process.

What happens after, who supports the modernized system?

You do, and that's the point. The system is yours: your architecture, your standards, your IP. Your team owns and evolves it, with the living model kept current so the understanding compounds instead of decaying. Gallop stays available for the next system, not as a permanent dependency.

Which LLMs does this use, am I locked into one vendor's model?

No lock-in. Gallop orchestrates the best model for each task and stays model-agnostic, commercial or open-source, today's leading models or tomorrow's. As the frontier moves, your platform moves with it.

Is my system too large or too complex for this?

Gallop is built for the most complex estates: systems with hundreds of millions of lines, decades of history, hundreds of integrations. Enterprises have already entrusted Gallop with millions lines of code.
